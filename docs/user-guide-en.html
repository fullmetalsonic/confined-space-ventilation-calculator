Example used throughout this guide

Scenario: a simple rectangular space, 3 m long × 2.5 m wide × 2 m high; no identified continuous contaminant source; three workers; South Korea · KOSHA H-80-2021 profile.

InputExample valueMeaning
Space volume15 m³3 × 2.5 × 2
Initial purge multiplier10Planned air exchanges before work starts
Continuous air changes20 ACHAir exchanges per hour during work
Blower rated flow · efficiency500 m³/h · 75%Applied airflow in the plan is 375 m³/h

1. Set the safety profile first

Under Safety jurisdiction profile, select the rule set that applies at the workplace. For this example, select South Korea · KOSHA H-80-2021.

UI language changes reading language only; it does not select the law. Leave print paper as A4 and display units as SI for this example.

Check: Verify the selected country, profile, review date, and the official-source link in the profile guidance.

Example start screen: confirm profile, paper size, and units first.

2. Choose the calculation method

A

General confined space / volume exchange — used in this example

Select this when no continuous contaminant source is identified. The calculator uses space volume and air-change criteria to calculate initial purge and continuous airflow.

B

Known generation rate

Use only when a gas or vapor hourly usage or generation rate is known from reliable data. Do not enter a guessed rate.

C

Unknown generation rate

Use when measured concentrations are available after operating the blower for a known interval. Review measurement conditions and the reliability warning with the result.

Example input: select General confined space, then choose Next: Volume.

3. Build the 15 m³ space volume

Select a rectangular space and enter length 3 m, width 2.5 m, and height 2 m. The calculated effective volume should show 15.00 m³.

Subtract fixed items that displace air and add connected areas that must be ventilated together. A wrong volume makes every later result wrong.

Check: compare 15.00 m³ with the drawing or field measurement before moving to Work conditions.

4. Enter work conditions

Enter 3 workers. This is a record field; it does not change the volume-exchange airflow equation in this example.

Screen fieldExample inputWhat to verify
Initial purge multiplier10Air exchanges planned before work starts. Do not lower it without a profile- and risk-assessment-based justification.
Continuous air changes20 ACHThe hourly criterion that must be maintained while work continues.

Check: move to the Results step after confirming both entries.

5. Read the two airflow results separately

The example produces two different values:

ResultCalculationExample value
Initial purge volume15 m³ space × 10 exchanges150.00 m³
Continuous airflow during work15 m³ space × 20 ACH300.00 m³/h

Initial purge volume is the pre-entry air quantity. Continuous airflow is the hourly flow to maintain while work is under way. They are not interchangeable.

Next: use the minimum required continuous airflow, 300.00 m³/h, for blower matching.

6. Match a blower and check reserve margin

Enter an equipment name such as 500 m³/h axial blower. Select Rated airflow × efficiency, then enter rated airflow 500 m³/h, efficiency 75%, and planned quantity 1.

ItemCalculationExample value
Applied airflow500 × 75%375.00 m³/h
Reserve margin(375 − 300) ÷ 300 × 10025.0%

If there is a manufacturer operating point or field-measured value, choose the airflow basis that matches that evidence. Check explosion protection, duct loss, and actual installation separately.

Check: confirm that status is OK and the planned quantity is not below the required quantity.

7. Check before printing the report

  • Does the selected profile apply to the actual workplace?
  • Do the volume and blower-airflow evidence match the field data?
  • Have initial purge volume and continuous airflow been kept separate?
  • If supplementary languages are needed, have the correct print languages been selected?
  • Is it understood that this report is a planning attachment, not a replacement for atmospheric measurement?

Choose the report and any required supplemental-language pages in the print screen. The official legal source and site procedure take precedence over translations.
